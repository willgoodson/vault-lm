# Cellular Respiration

Cells break down glucose to release energy as **ATP**, the cell's energy currency.

**C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ → 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + ATP**

## Stages
| Stage | Where | ATP made (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Glycolysis | Cytoplasm | 2 |
| Krebs (citric acid) cycle | Mitochondrial matrix | 2 |
| Electron transport chain | Inner mitochondrial membrane | ~26–28 |

In total, one glucose yields about **30–32 ATP** (older textbooks say 36–38).

## Without oxygen
When oxygen runs short, cells fall back on **fermentation**, which yields only the 2 ATP from glycolysis:
- In muscles: glucose → lactic acid.
- In yeast: glucose → ethanol + CO₂.

The mitochondria where this happens are described in [[Organelles]]; the reverse process is [[Photosynthesis]].
