# Photosynthesis

Plants, algae and some bacteria turn light energy into chemical energy inside [[Organelles|chloroplasts]].

**6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂**

## Two stages
1. **Light-dependent reactions** (thylakoid membranes): chlorophyll absorbs light, water is split, oxygen is released, and ATP and NADPH are made.
2. **Calvin cycle** (stroma): ATP and NADPH power the fixing of CO₂ into glucose, with the enzyme **RuBisCO**.

## Limiting factors
Light intensity, CO₂ concentration and temperature each limit the rate; whichever is in shortest supply sets the pace.

Photosynthesis is roughly the reverse of [[Cellular Respiration]].
