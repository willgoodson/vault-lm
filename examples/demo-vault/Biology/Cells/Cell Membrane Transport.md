# Cell Membrane Transport

The plasma membrane is **selectively permeable**: small non-polar molecules (O₂, CO₂) cross easily, while ions and large polar molecules need help.

## Passive transport (no energy needed)
- **Diffusion**: molecules move from high to low concentration.
- **Facilitated diffusion**: the same, but through channel or carrier proteins.
- **Osmosis**: diffusion of water across a membrane toward the side with more dissolved solute.

## Active transport (needs ATP)
- **Pumps** move substances *against* their concentration gradient. The sodium–potassium pump moves 3 Na⁺ out and 2 K⁺ in for each ATP used.
- **Endocytosis** and **exocytosis** move large particles in and out in vesicles.

The ATP for active transport comes from [[Cellular Respiration]].
